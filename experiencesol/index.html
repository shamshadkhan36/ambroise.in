What is included in the ₹1.8 Lakh all-inclusive price? ▾
The package includes custom 3D design concept, workshop fabrication of wooden/paint structures, raised wooden floor with carpet, LED lighting fixtures, basic furniture (reception desk & meeting table), electricity wiring, transport to venue, on-site installation, and post-show dismantling.
How fast can Ambroise provide a 3D booth design concept? ▾
Our 3D design team delivers the first comprehensive 3D render proposal with multiple angle views within 48 to 72 hours of receiving your booth size and design brief.

Yes! We coordinate directly with event authorities and venue safety teams for structural stability certificates, electrical load permissions, height permissions, and insurance compliance.
